## Export Notes

1. Click on Notebook on the dashboard.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Export_001.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Notebook tab on top toolbar.' title='' width='800' height='' />

2. Select one or more messages, or select all.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Export_002.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Checking off notes.' title='' width='800' height='' />

3. Click the Export button.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Export_003.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Export button on Notebook toolbar.' title='' width='800' height='' />

4. Select either all or a selection of notes, and then select a file type. <img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Export_004.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Picking export options: selected note, and changing the file type.' title='' width='600' height='' />
5. You can select CSV (for a spreadsheet that opens in Excel), Word Doc, or PDF. After making your selection, click Export.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Export_005.png' | relative_url }}' alt='File type dropdown menu with CSV, word doc, PDF.' title='' width='800' height='' />

6. Your browser will download the relevant file!  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Export_006.png' | relative_url }}' alt='PDF output with one page per note.' title='' width='800' height='' />
