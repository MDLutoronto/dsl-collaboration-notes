## Add Notes

You can add notes from most places in the Digital Scholar Lab, and your Notes page will show where you created those notes, e.g. whether while you are building a collection or running an analysis.

1. Let's say that you wanted to share notes with your collaborators on how you built a collection of texts. On the top toolbar, click on Build.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_001.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Build option on main toolbar.' title='' width='800' height='' />
2. For the purposes of this example, let's say you're working on a project about the history of coffee. Under Search, type Coffee and press enter.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_002.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Running a basic search for coffee.' title='' width='800' height='' />

    *Note: you can consult much more complex search strategies, including uploading texts, in our [instructions on building text collections](https://mdl.library.utoronto.ca/technology/tutorials/digital-scholar-lab-collections).*
3. Click on the Add Note button.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_003.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Add Note icon on secondary toolbar.' title='' width='800' height='' />

4. Add a title - say, "First search" - and some text, "Basic search, coffee."  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_004.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Adding title and text.' title='' width='800' height='' />

5. Optionally, click that you want this note to be anonymous.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_005.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Anonymizing your comment.' title='' width='800' height='' />

6. Click Save.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_006.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Saving the note.' title='' width='800' height='' />

7. There will be a popup saying that the note has been saved. Click Close.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_007.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Confirmation popup.' title='' width='800' height='' />

8. But you can also make notes on individual texts. Click on the title of the first text in your search. (For me, that's "Brazilian Coffee Situation" but yours might be different.)  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_008.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Selecting first search result.' title='' width='800' height='' />

9. In this document view, click on Add Note. Repeat the above steps to create a second note.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_009.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Adding note while in the doc viewer.' title='' width='800' height='' />

10. This time, when you create the note, click "See all notes in Notebook."  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_010.1.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Note saved popup.' title='' width='800' height='' />

    Alternatively, you can always access your workspace's notes by click on "Notebook" on the top toolbar.  
        <img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_010.2.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Notebook button on main dashboard.' title='' width='800' height='' />
11. Here you can view all notes (whether you or another group member created them), filter them by date, location (i.e. where in the Digital Scholar Lab they were created), by author, and by additional filters, including free text for search terms.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_011.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Notebook view with notes listing different locations.' title='' width='800' height='' />

    *Note: once you have [run an analysis tool](https://mdl.library.utoronto.ca/technology/tutorials/digital-scholar-lab-analysis), you can create notes that save visualizations of specific runs. This is great for comparing different results and for documenting your findings.*
12. Borrowing [an example from later in this tutorial](https://mdl.library.utoronto.ca/technology/tutorials/digital-scholar-lab-analysis#topic_modeling), once you have run a tool, click on Add Note on the results page.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_012.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Topic modeling output with the Add Note button.' title='' width='800' height='' />

13. Add a title and text as before, then click on the Capture Analysis Image button to add a screenshot of your analysis results.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_013.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Analysis button.' title='' width='800' height='' />

14. Your note popup will now include an Analysis image in a new Attachments section. Click on Save.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_014.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Note now has an attachment with the analysis image.' title='' width='800' height='' />

15. On the top toolbar, click on Notebook. Your note will include a resizeable screenshot of your analysis results.  
<img src='{{ '/assets/images/DSL_Collaboration_Notes_015.png' | relative_url }}' alt='Notebook view with analysis results screenshot in top note.' title='' width='800' height='' />
